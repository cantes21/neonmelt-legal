Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NeonMelt: Color Tunnel Dash handles information when you use the game.

Publisher: Can Büyüklü, Türkiye
Privacy contact: mehmetcanbuyuklu@gmail.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game NeonMelt: Color Tunnel Dash (the “Game”), published by Can Büyüklü (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The Game is a general-audience arcade game and is not specifically directed to children.

2. Information we handle

We do not require an account and do not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, contacts or payment information. The current version of the Game does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, third-party crash reporting, tracking, cloud saves or other third-party data-collection SDKs.

Gameplay progress, high scores, settings and notification preferences may be stored locally on your device. The Game may schedule local notifications, which are processed on your device by iOS. This locally stored information is not transmitted to us by the Game.

If you voluntarily email support, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, device details and attachments.

3. How information is used

Locally stored information is used only on your device to run the Game, remember progress and settings, and schedule requested reminders. Information sent in a support email is used to investigate the issue, reply to you, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Support communications

Support is optional. When you contact us, processing is based on responding to your request, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the Game, and compliance with legal obligations where applicable. Email is delivered through the email services used by you and by us, which process messages under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Notifications and device controls

You can allow or refuse notification permission and later change it in iOS Settings > Notifications > NeonMelt. You can delete locally stored game information by deleting the Game from your device. The current version does not request Apple’s app-tracking permission.

6. Sharing and international processing

The Game does not transmit personal information to us or share it with advertising or analytics providers. If you contact support, your message is processed by the relevant email service providers and may be processed in countries other than your own under their applicable safeguards. We do not sell personal information.

7. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you reset or uninstall the Game. Support messages are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of information contained in your support correspondence by contacting us, subject to information we must retain by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to be informed, access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, request data portability, and complain to a competent data-protection authority. Residents of Türkiye may exercise the rights provided under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data. We may request reasonable information to verify and process a request.

9. Children

The Game is not specifically directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Security

Local game data is protected by the security features of your device and iOS. We use reasonable measures for support communications appropriate to an independent mobile game. No storage or electronic communication method is completely secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game, service providers or legal requirements change. If a future version adds advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud features or other data-processing services, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as appropriate. The revised policy will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email mehmetcanbuyuklu@gmail.com.
