Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NeonMelt: Color Tunnel Dash handles information when you use the game.

Publisher: Can Büyüklü, Türkiye
Privacy contact: mehmetcanbuyuklu@gmail.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game NeonMelt: Color Tunnel Dash (the “Game”), published by Can Büyüklü (“we”, “us”, or “our”). The Game is a general-audience arcade game and is not specifically directed to children.

2. Information we handle

We do not require an account and do not ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number or contacts. Gameplay settings and progress may be stored locally on your device.

The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on your device settings, location, consent choices and applicable law, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • device identifiers, including an advertising identifier where permission is granted;
  • advertising data and interactions with advertisements;
  • app interactions, diagnostic information, crash logs and performance data; and
  • consent and privacy-choice signals.

Google describes its data practices in Google’s Privacy Policy and its partner services information.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to operate and secure the Game, serve and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and abuse, remember privacy choices, diagnose technical issues and improve performance. Personalized advertising or cross-app tracking is used only where permitted and, where required, after obtaining your consent or device permission.

4. Legal grounds

Where applicable, processing is based on your consent, the performance of the service you request, compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention and basic service measurement, provided those interests do not override your rights. You may withdraw consent through the Game’s privacy options or relevant device settings.

5. Advertising choices

Where required, the Game displays a consent form before requesting advertisements. You can revisit available choices through the “Privacy Choices” control in the Game’s Settings screen when that control is required. On iOS, you can also manage tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Refusing personalized advertising does not prevent you from playing the Game, although non-personalized or limited advertisements may still appear.

6. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by Google and participating advertising, measurement, security and infrastructure providers for the purposes described above. These providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable legal safeguards and their respective privacy terms. We do not receive money in exchange for personal information. Certain advertising disclosures may nevertheless be treated as a “sale” or “sharing” under some privacy laws; where required, you can exercise the available opt-out through the Game’s privacy choices.

7. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you reset the Game’s data or uninstall it. Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to their policies and legal obligations. You may contact us about a privacy request using the email above. Because the Game has no user account, we may be unable to identify information held only under a device or advertising identifier.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to be informed, access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, request data portability, and complain to a competent data-protection authority. Residents of Türkiye may exercise the rights provided under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data. We may request reasonable information to verify and process a request.

9. Children

The Game is not specifically directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to an independent mobile game. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game, service providers or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email mehmetcanbuyuklu@gmail.com.
