Common questions

How do I change advertising privacy choices?

Open the Game’s Settings screen and select “Privacy Choices” when that option is available. iOS tracking permission can also be changed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How do I delete locally stored game data?

Delete the Game from your device. If cloud or account features are introduced in a future version, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated with additional instructions.

I found a bug or the Game crashed.

Restart the Game and make sure iOS and the Game are updated. If the issue continues, send the device and version information listed above.
